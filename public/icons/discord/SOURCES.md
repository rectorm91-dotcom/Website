## Availability icons

These are pixel crops of the original status symbols in Discord's [official status reference](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/211374998-What-do-those-colored-dots-next-to-my-avatar-mean), not redrawn approximations. Each crop is the 14×14 region at x=28, y=30 in the source 240×52 image. No text or other avatar from the reference is displayed.
